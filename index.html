    How labeling works

    The pretrained model auto-suggests boxes for faces and 80 common object classes (phone, keyboard, mouse, bottle, cup…). For anything it doesn't know — a yoghurt box, a pen, a specific product — drag on the captured image to draw a box yourself and type any label you want. Rename or delete suggested boxes freely. Every saved capture grows a dataset we can later use to train a custom detector that recognizes your items directly.

    All processing happens locally in your browser. No images are uploaded to any server.
