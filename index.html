    What can it detect?

    Faces (via MediaPipe) plus the 80 COCO object classes, including: cell phone, mouse, keyboard, laptop, bottle, cup, book, scissors, remote, tv, clock, backpack, chair, person and more. Custom items (e.g. pens or specific products/brands) require a custom-trained model — planned as a future phase.

    All processing happens locally in your browser. No images are uploaded to any server.
